1. Who we are

InTime is a mobile game created and published by Ans, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom ("we", "us", "our"). For the purposes of data protection law, we are the controller of the limited personal information processed through the game, except where our advertising partners act as independent controllers of the information they collect (see section 9).

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the InTime game on Android and iOS, including any updates, and to this web page. It does not cover the websites, apps or services of third parties, such as Google Play, the Apple App Store or the advertisers whose ads appear in the game. Those are governed by their own privacy policies.

3. Information kept on your device

The game saves the following on your phone or tablet so you can carry on where you left off:

  • game progress: unlocked levels, best results and the current level;
  • in-game items: coins, keys, power boosters, characters you own and the character you use;
  • rewards: daily reward streak, Lucky Spin usage and timers;
  • settings: music, sound effects, volume, vibration, steering sensitivity, graphics quality, battery saver, whether the tutorial has been completed, and your privacy choice;
  • a record of purchases that don't run out (such as Remove Ads), so they can be restored.

This information stays on your device. We do not receive a copy of it, we cannot see it, and we cannot recover it if it is lost. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. If your device backs up app data to your Google or Apple account, that backup is handled by Google or Apple under their own terms.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or camera input, or precise GPS location. The game does not have chat, user profiles, leaderboards with personal names, or any way for players to contact one another.

5. Your advertising choices

The first time you open InTime, we ask how you would like ads to work:

  • Accept: our partners may use your advertising identifier and the information above to show ads that match your interests.
  • No personalised ads: ads are still shown, but they are not based on your interests or past activity. We also tell partners that your information must not be sold or shared for cross-context advertising.

No ads are requested until you have made a choice. You can change your mind at any time in Settings > Privacy, and the change takes effect straight away. On iPhone and iPad, the game uses the IDFA only if you allow it in Apple's "Allow tracking" prompt, and you can change this later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads (the path may differ slightly by device). Buying Remove Ads stops interstitial ads; rewarded ads remain available because you choose to watch them.

6. In-app purchases

You can buy optional items such as coin and key packs and Remove Ads. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive or store your name, card number, billing address or other payment details. The store tells the game only that a purchase was completed, together with a transaction reference, so the item can be delivered and, for purchases that don't run out, restored on a new device using Restore Purchases.

8. Why we use information (legal bases)

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, the legal bases for processing are:

  • Consent for personalised advertising and for reading or storing advertising identifiers where consent is required. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings > Privacy; this does not affect processing that happened before.
  • Legitimate interests for showing non-personalised ads, frequency capping, measuring ad delivery and preventing fraud, which keep the game free. We have balanced these interests against your rights and keep the information involved to a minimum.
  • Performance of a contract for delivering and restoring items you buy.
  • Legal obligation where we must keep records, for example for tax purposes.

10. International transfers

Our partners may process information in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where information from the UK, EEA or Switzerland is transferred, partners rely on recognised safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, or the EU-US, UK-US and Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework.

11. How long information is kept

  • Information on your device: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Information collected by ad partners: for the periods set out in their privacy policies, generally no longer than needed for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Emails you send us: up to two years after your question is resolved, then deleted.

13. Security

The game and its partners communicate over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Because we do not run our own servers or hold a player database, there is very little personal information in our hands to protect. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we and our partners take reasonable steps to protect information.

14. Children

InTime is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age for consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the game in a way that involved their personal information, please contact us and we will help, including by asking our partners to delete it.

15. Your rights (UK, EEA and Switzerland)

You have the right to: access the personal information held about you; have it corrected; have it deleted; restrict or object to its processing (including objecting to direct marketing at any time); receive it in a portable format; and withdraw consent. Because we hold almost no information ourselves, most requests are best handled by changing your choice in Settings > Privacy, resetting your advertising ID, or contacting the partners listed above. We are happy to help with any of these. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the EEA it is the authority in your country.

16. Your rights (United States)

Residents of California and other states with consumer privacy laws (such as Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas and Oregon) may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to access and delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its "sale", "sharing" or use for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Choosing No personalised ads in Settings > Privacy opts you out of targeted advertising and the "sharing" of your information for that purpose. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. Categories collected in the last 12 months: identifiers (advertising ID, IP address), internet and device activity (ad interactions), and approximate geolocation, all as described in section 4.

17. Your rights (other regions)

If you live elsewhere, for example in Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA), Australia or Japan, you may have similar rights to access, correct and delete your information and to withdraw consent. Contact us and we will respond in line with the law that applies to you.

18. Do Not Track

The game has no web browsing features. Your choice in Settings > Privacy is the control that applies to advertising in the game.

19. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or the law changes. The effective date at the top will always show the latest version. If a change significantly affects how your information is used, we will tell you in the game and, where required, ask for your consent again.

20. Glossary

Advertising identifier: a resettable ID supplied by your phone's operating system for advertising. It is not your phone number or device serial number. Mediation: a service that picks which ad network fills each ad slot. Personalised ads: ads chosen using information about your interests or past activity. Non-personalised ads: ads chosen using only general context, such as the app and approximate location.
